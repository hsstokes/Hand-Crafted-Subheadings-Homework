What To Do

1
Choose Your Words

Think about what title you want for your portfolio page:

  • Pick short words (1-2 words is best)
  • Choose words that match your project theme
  • Write down which letters you need

Good examples: "FOUND", "TEXTURE", "COLOUR", "MAKING", "PROCESS"

2
Get Your Template

Download a template with boxes for your letters:

  • Go to Calligraphr.com
  • Log in (use your account from Session 1)
  • Click "Templates"
  • Choose only the letters you need
  • Download as PDF
  • Print on white paper
Go to Calligraphr →
3
Get Your Materials

Get what you need for your chosen method:

🎨 What You Need

  • Letter stamps: Stamps + ink pad (black or colour)
  • Foam stamps: Foam sheets + scissors + glue + cardboard + ink or paint
  • Cut and stick: Old magazines + scissors + glue stick
  • Drawing: Pens, pencils, or markers
  • Painting: Brushes + paint (acrylic or watercolour)
4
Make Your Letters

Put one letter in each box on your template:

  • Keep each letter inside its box
  • Make letters with interesting texture
  • Mistakes are OK — they make it look handmade
  • Wait for ink or paint to dry completely
5
Scan Your Template

Make a digital copy of your finished template:

  • Use a scanner at 300 DPI (high quality)
  • Or use a phone app (Adobe Scan or Microsoft Lens)
  • Save as JPEG or PNG
  • Check it is clear and straight
⚠️

Important: Stamps Are Backwards!

When you use stamps, the letter on the stamp is the wrong way round. This is normal!

  • If you make your own stamp, draw the letter backwards
  • Test on paper first before using the template
  • Press hard and flat for a good print
💡

Tips for Good Results

  • Texture is good: Uneven ink and brush marks look interesting
  • Dark on white: Dark letters on white paper scan best
  • Protect your table: Put newspaper under your work
  • Make extras: Make 2-3 of each letter so you can choose the best one
  • Wait to dry: Make sure everything is fully dry before scanning

💭 Think About Your Style

Before you start, think about how you want your letters to look:

  • Do you want bold and strong or soft and delicate?
  • Does your method match your project theme?
  • How will these letters look with your other portfolio work?
